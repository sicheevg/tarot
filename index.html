Free Marseille Tarot reading online — the Jodorowsky method

A free online reading of the Marseille Tarot (Tarot de Marseille), built around the Jodorowsky Tarot method. Ask a question, tap Read and get a detailed seven-card reading of the Tarot of Marseille. Three cards are drawn — origin, centre and direction — and four more are derived by adding their numbers (the “theosophical sum” spread associated with Alejandro Jodorowsky).

The interpretation follows the ideas of The Way of Tarot by Alejandro Jodorowsky and Marianne Costa: the ten-degree numerology of the Major Arcana, pairs of cards ten apart and pairs adding up to 21, the direction in which the figures look, the voice of each card, a family-tree question and a safe symbolic “psychomagic” step. The reading describes the present rather than predicting the future.

All 22 Major Arcana of the historic Jean Dodal deck (c. 1701, public domain): The Fool, The Magician, The High Priestess, The Empress, The Emperor, The Pope, The Lovers, The Chariot, Justice, The Hermit, The Wheel of Fortune, Strength, The Hanged Man, the Nameless Arcanum, Temperance, The Devil, The Tower, The Star, The Moon, The Sun, Judgement and The World.

Free, no sign-up, no ads, works offline on iPhone, Android and desktop; add it to your home screen. Available in English and Russian.

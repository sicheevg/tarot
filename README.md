# Tarot of Marseille — free online reading (Jodorowsky method)

**Open the app: https://sicheevg.github.io/tarot/** · [Русская версия](https://sicheevg.github.io/tarot/ru/)

A free, offline-capable web app for reading the Tarot of Marseille. Ask a question, tap **Read** and get a detailed seven-card reading inspired by *The Way of Tarot* by Alejandro Jodorowsky and Marianne Costa: the theosophical-sum spread, ten-degree numerology, card pairs, the direction of the figures' gazes, the voice of each card and a safe symbolic step.

- Free, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking; works offline on iPhone, Android and desktop (add to home screen).
- English and Russian.
- Historic Jean Dodal deck (c. 1701, public domain, Wikimedia Commons).
